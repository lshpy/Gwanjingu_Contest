# Gwangjin-gu Through the News: LDA Topic Modelling and Named-Entity Analysis

> Competition entry by team ku-optimizer (Seunghyun Lee): an analysis of news coverage of Gwangjin-gu, Seoul, to find its main issues and key people, organizations and places.

Original title (Korean): 뉴스로 살펴본 광진구의 주요 이슈와 핵심 인물-기관-지역: LDA토픽모델링과 개체명 분석을 통해

## Summary

News articles about Gwangjin-gu were collected by web scraping, processed with morphological analysis and keyword
extraction, and analysed with LDA topic modelling and named-entity recognition (NER). The report interprets the
extracted topics and entities and proposes directions for the district's development.
